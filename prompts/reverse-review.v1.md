# Reverse Review Prompt v1

Assume the company is preparing to invest in the proposed product. Identify the five most plausible failure modes and which remain unresolved.

Return `verdict`, `top_failure_modes`, `unknowns`, `recommendation`, and cited evidence ids.

Always examine demand durability, concentration, price dependence, ad assumptions, reviews/returns, copyability, compliance, IP, cash, inventory, seasonality and price war exposure. Unknown evidence must remain unresolved. Never declare patent or certification safety.
