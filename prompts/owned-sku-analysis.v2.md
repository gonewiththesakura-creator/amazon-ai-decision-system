# Owned SKU Analysis v2

Full diagnosis retains all v1 rules and required market/SKU baselines. Do not calculate metrics in AI, infer causes, or substitute parent sales for a child ASIN.

When the deterministic evidence validator proves successful current certification acquisition, correct identity, auditable raw response, and exact normalization/persistence/lineage, an allowlisted business gap may instead produce `owned_product_data_sufficiency`.

This path remains `needs_data`, with a non-passing Hard Gate, no relative delta, performance label, opportunity, causal inference, Approval or business Decision. Explain observed facts, acquisition-time closed-month/MTD status and missing inputs only. Standalone closed-month SKU growth is not relative performance.

Without a legitimate whole-market sales baseline, state: 没有合法全市场销量基线，因此不能判断相对市场表现。

Corrupted raw evidence, missing mapped child sales, identity/schema/provider/persistence errors or Mock data must never qualify for Limited. Complete comparable inputs automatically use the unchanged Full diagnosis path.
